# Breed Wild / Domestic Classification

## Definitions

| Category | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Wild | Best suited to natural herds away from villages, farms, and stables |
| Domestic | Best suited to villages, farms, stables, settlements, breeding, or trading |
| Both | Plausible in free-roaming herds and in human-influenced contexts |

## Design note

These categories are for gameplay design, not strict biology.

Most modern horse breeds are domestic. The "wild" category here really means "good fit for natural, free-roaming, wilderness-style herds."
